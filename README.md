# 🐈 About Me :

🧑‍💼 Statistician at Dares, Statistics department of the French Labour Ministery.

I am working on the labour conditions impacts on health using the **Conditions de Travail** survey and **SNDS** data (Medical consumption).

🏢 Member of [**@InseeFr**](https://github.com/InseeFr) civil servants.

🌐 Member of the [Institut Santé Numérique en Société](https://isns.fr/membre/leopold-maurice/) (Numeric Heath in Society Institute).

📈 Rstats enthousiast. 

💾 Advocating in favor of opensource.

🗺️ Love interactive dataviz, in particular maps.

🍵 Tea, ☕ coffee, 🧉 yerba mate addict.

📔 Interest in socio-history of statistics.

Check my [**website**](https://leomaurice.github.com) for more infos !
